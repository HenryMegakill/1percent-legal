You can delete your account and all its data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open 1% and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Account & privacy.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your profile, anonymous name, daily history, posts, replies, stars, likes, reports and blocks from our servers, immediately. Proof photos are only stored on your phone and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Without the app

Email henrydang344@gmail.com from the email linked to your account with the subject Delete my 1% account. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What we keep

Nothing tied to you. Aggregated, anonymous statistics (for example total daily active users) are not linked to any account.
