Privacy Policy

1% · Last updated November 11, 2026

Who we are

1% is made by Henry Dang. Contact: henrydang344@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your anonymous name, your goals, and your daily results (points, glasses of water, step count, workout minutes, focus sessions, social media minutes per app). If you add an email to keep your account, we store that email. Posts, replies, stars, likes, reports and blocks you make in the community.

What stays on your phone

Bedtime and workout proof photos stay on your device. We only record that a photo was taken. The list of other apps you use and their exact usage stays on your phone; only the daily total and per-app minutes for supported social apps are used for your score.

Permissions

Camera: bedtime check-ins and workout proof photos. Physical activity: counting steps. Health Connect (Android, optional): reading your step count for today, so steps you take while 1% is closed still count. Usage access: measuring time in social media apps for the Social media pillar. Notifications: reminders you can turn off in Settings. Each is optional; without it the matching feature does not earn points.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to check which plan you have. It receives your anonymous account id and your purchase history, not your name or email.

Health Connect

If you connect Health Connect, 1% reads only your step count, and only for the current day. We use it to score the Exercise pillar and store the daily total with your results, like steps counted by the app itself. We never read other health data, never write to Health Connect, and never sell, share or use health data for ads. You can disconnect any time in Health Connect settings or Profile > Account & privacy. The use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

How we use it

To calculate your daily points, streaks and duel scores, to show your posts to other members under your anonymous name, and to keep the community safe (reviewing reports). We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who can see what

Other members see only your anonymous name, league, posts, replies and star counts. They never see your email, your health data or your photos.

Service providers

Data is stored with Supabase (database and authentication). Payments, when available, are handled by Google Play.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in Profile > Account & privacy > Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, history, posts, replies, stars and reports from our servers within 30 days of the request at most (usually immediately).

Children

1% is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
